# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, use evidence when discussing model performance, disclose limitations, avoid deceptive claims, and protect private data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publication of private information, intentional data leakage, fabricated validation, or promotion of illegal or underage gambling is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issues, comments, or access that violate this code. Serious concerns should be reported privately through the process in `SECURITY.md`.
